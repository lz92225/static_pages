岛上闪念隐私政策

生效日期为【YYYY 年 MM 月 DD 日】。

本政策适用于由【开发者或运营主体全称】提供的岛上闪念（IslandMemo）iOS App 及其组件。使用 App 无需创建岛上闪念账号。

闪念与剪贴板

你保存的文字、链接、标签、保存时间、录入方式和处理状态保存在设备上，用于在收件箱中显示、搜索、整理和导出闪念。删除闪念后，App 会清空记录中的文字、标签和录入方式，保留保存时间用于计算当日额度。

点击灵动岛的分类按钮会打开对应速存页。你在速存页或手动输入页点系统「粘贴」时，App 才接收剪贴板中的文字或链接。速记页可检查剪贴板是否有可粘贴的类型，以显示粘贴入口。iOS 可能要求你确认是否允许粘贴。你也可以直接手动输入。

开启收集模式后，系统可在灵动岛或锁屏的实时活动中显示今日保存数量及最近一条闪念的摘要。你可以在 App 内结束收集，或在系统设置中管理实时活动权限。

iCloud 与购买

在支持 iCloud 同步的版本中，解锁 Pro 并使用已启用 iCloud 的 Apple 账户时，App 可通过 CloudKit 私有数据库在同一账户的设备间同步闪念。你可以通过 iOS 的 iCloud 设置管理 App 对 iCloud 的使用。iCloud 服务对数据的处理遵循 Apple 的政策。 Apple 账户与隐私

Pro 购买和恢复购买由 App Store 处理。App 通过 StoreKit 验证购买结果，并在设备上保存用于识别 Pro 权益的状态。支付信息由 Apple 处理。 App Store 与隐私

提醒事项、导出与外部链接

你选择「转提醒事项」后，App 会请求提醒事项权限，并在获得授权后将选中的内容和你确认的提醒时间写入系统提醒事项。你可以随时在 iOS 的隐私设置中撤回该权限，已创建的待办可在提醒事项内管理。

导出文件在设备上生成，由你通过系统分享菜单选择接收方。点击已保存的链接会打开相应网站或 App。分享后的副本及外部网站或 App 对数据的处理取决于接收方。

数据管理与联系

App 无开发者运营的闪念存储服务器、广告或追踪 SDK。你可以在收件箱或归档中删除闪念，在系统设置中管理 iCloud 与各项权限，以及自行删除已导出的文件。

IslandMemo Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Month DD, YYYY].

This policy applies to the IslandMemo iOS app and its components, provided by [Full legal name of the developer or operator]. You can use the app without creating an IslandMemo account.

Memos and the Clipboard

The text, links, tags, save times, input methods, and processing status you save are stored on your device so you can view, search, organize, and export your memos from the Inbox. When you delete a memo, the app clears its text, tags, and input method. It keeps the save time to calculate your daily quota.

Selecting a category in the Dynamic Island opens the corresponding Quick Save screen. The app receives text or links from your clipboard when you tap the system Paste button on the Quick Save or manual entry screen. Quick Save may check whether the clipboard contains a supported data type to decide whether to show Paste. iOS may ask you to allow pasting. You can also enter text manually.

While Collection Mode is active, an iOS Live Activity may show today’s save count and a summary of your most recent memo in the Dynamic Island or on the Lock Screen. You can end the session in the app or manage Live Activities permissions in iOS Settings.

iCloud and Purchases

In versions that support iCloud sync, if you unlock Pro and use an Apple Account with iCloud enabled, the app can sync memos between devices signed in to that account using Apple’s private CloudKit database. You can manage the app’s access to iCloud in iOS Settings. Apple handles data in iCloud under its own policies. Apple Account & Privacy

The App Store handles Pro purchases and restorations. The app verifies purchases with StoreKit and stores the information needed to recognize your Pro entitlement on your device. Apple handles payment information. App Store & Privacy

Reminders, Exports, and External Links

When you choose to add a memo to Reminders, the app requests access. If you grant access, it writes the selected content and the reminder time you confirm to Apple’s Reminders app. You can revoke access in iOS privacy settings and manage existing reminders in Reminders.

The app creates export files on your device. You choose where to send them using the system share sheet. Opening a saved link takes you to the corresponding website or app. Recipients determine how they handle shared copies, and each external website or app handles data according to its own practices.

Managing Your Data and Contacting Us

The app has no developer-operated memo storage servers, ads, or tracking SDKs. You can delete memos from the Inbox or Archive, manage iCloud and app permissions in iOS Settings, and delete exported files yourself.

For privacy questions, or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you previously sent to support, email [gulugltop@163.com]. When you contact us by email, we use your email address and the information you provide to handle your request.
